CRITIC (score 78/100, wowed=False): Solid, well-engineered and almost fully compliant, but the result is "nice", not AAA. The good parts: the overlapping white card bridges night to paper cleanly, the countdown is calm and correct (3 d 13 t 59 min from fredag kl. 10.00, Danish singular/plural labels, tabular Philosopher digits, downward rolling drum), the dotted dividers and meter rail are on-brand, the discs are flat and never rotated, ® is present, there are no em-dashes or medical claims, and the Førpris note uses the § 9 a wording. What keeps it from AAA: (1) The WebGL bubbles fight the type. On mobile they sit on top of the lead copy, the "59" digit box and the "Slutter …" line. On desktop they sit inside the DAGE and SEK boxes and get hard-clipped at the band's top and right edges. (2) The bubbles read as stock soap-bubble overlays (clear ring plus white specular) rather than amber oil or serum drops, and 12 of them is "busy", not "a few". (3) The copy repeats itself: every offer is said 3–4 times (disc, eyebrow, title, body, then the meter line again). (4) Column rhythm breaks because tile 1's title wraps to two lines while the others don't, and tile 3 has a big empty gap. (5) The muted small text fails 4.5:1 contrast, and the card's deep shadow muddies the paper. The section also doesn't express the "i ro og mag" idea anywhere; "Tre fordele" is generic. Side by side with Aesop or Apple, this reads as a very competent template, not a signature moment.
