CRITIC (score 79/100, wowed=False): This is well engineered, but it is not AAA yet. The code is the strongest part. The FLIP filter is careful, tilt uses exponential critical damping, easing is viscous, the fly-to-cart path is a cubic arc with an oil trail and droplets, the quick view uses a real <dialog>, the dotted leaders follow the dotted-line rule, the Førpris wording is correct, and the console is clean. Side by side with Rhode or Aesop, though, the screenshots read as a tidy Shopify grid, not a crafted Black Week moment.

The biggest problem is a product-truth error that would fail the brand checklist. The Komplet Pakken packshot shows 3 products, but the card lists 4: Natcreme is missing from the photo.

There are also visible composition faults:
- The Komplet Pakken photographed floor bleeds into the card's left edge.
- The desktop "Hurtig visning" pill sits on top of the bottles and contact shadow.
- Sampakke packs render at about twice the scale of the same bottles in the single cards next to them.
- The featured cards' price and button rows don't line up with their row neighbours.
- About 200px of the gift tile is dead space, holding two overlapping bubbles that repeat the eyebrow and the body copy.

Mobile drops the claim (a defensible trade-off, but it breaks the spec). Mobile also uses 9–9.5px uppercase type for eyebrows and meter labels, and the gift eyebrow wraps awkwardly.

On accessibility, the chip counts fail contrast (about 2.2:1), and the add buttons fail WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name.

The 3D work is hard to judge. f1 and f2 are almost identical to the first frame. The soft-light sheen over white does very little, so the "moving light" barely reads, and nothing here gives a glass or oil sensation. Fix the must-fixes below and this could land around 88–90. Getting to 92+ needs a real signature moment, such as a more visible light or glass sheen on the packs.

Use visible text plus a hidden suffix, e.g. <span>Læg i kurv</span><span class="nc-sr"> – ${name}</span>. [deals.css @media (max-width:760px) .deals-card:not(.deals-card--set) .deals-card__claim] -> Show the claim at 12.5px with -webkit-line-clamp: 2, or show a short claim (add a claimShort field in data via a shared request).
