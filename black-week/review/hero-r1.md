Side by side with Aesop or Apple, the oil is what gives it away as CG. Other problems: decorative 3D bubbles drift behind the DOM −25% disc and the price card. MUST FIX:
1. Oil pool looks like an opaque brown plastic coin. It has a hard dark rim and a dark inner band, with no translucency or depth. This is the single cheapest-looking element and kills the premium viscous oil idea. [src/sections/hero/scene.js ~L418-482 (oilPool material, poolGeometry(0.82, POOL_H), halo decals)] -> Make it a thin translucent film. Use MeshPhysicalMaterial with transmission ~0.9, roughness 0.02, ior 1.47, thickness ~0.08, amber attenuationColor (#c98a2a) with attenuationDistance ~0.3, and clearcoat 1. Feather the alpha or film thickness to 0 over the outer 15-20% of the radius so there is no hard rim. Remove the dark inner band. Make it wider and flatter (R ~1.1, H ≤0.02) so it reads as a spreading meniscus, with one sharp key-light glint streak and a warm caustic decal under it.
2. The falling drop is a matte, emissive orange bead (emissiveIntensity 0.9, transmission 0.55). It reads as a solid ball, not a golden oil drop. [src/sections/hero/scene.js L292-293 (oilDrop material)] -> Set transmission 0.95, emissiveIntensity ≤0.2, thickness ~0.6 and attenuationDistance ~0.35 so the drop refracts the backdrop with a bright inner caustic core and a dark refracted rim. Keep clearcoat for a crisp specular point. Add a subtle bloom catch on the highlight only.
3. Pipette reads as a solid orange crayon. The glass tube walls are invisible, and the oil column ends in a blunt flat cut at the tip, with no glass taper beyond it. [src/sections/hero/scene.js ~L368-396 (pipette/pipOil, glass material L280)] -> Give the tube visible glass. Raise thickness to ~0.6, add a fresnel or edge highlight (higher envMapIntensity plus a slightly larger BackSide shell for rim lines), and let the tube taper to a narrow glass nozzle about 8-10% longer than the oil. Give the oil a rounded meniscus at the top and a thin tapered tip where the drop neck forms.
4. Decorative 3D bubbles drift behind the DOM −25% disc and the price tag card. A bubble edge peeks out above the berry disc (desktop.png ~1110,170), another sits behind the card (desktop-f2 ~1165,490), and on mobile one hides at the right edge behind −25%. It reads as a layering accident. [scene.js bubble sim (~L683-757) + hero.js onFrame] -> Pass the overlay screen rects (bubble centre + radius, tag card rect) into the scene each frame. Treat them as avoidance zones in the bubble flow (same no-bounce velocity removal used for the bottle capsule), or fade a bubble's alpha to 0 within r+24px of an overlay.
5. Label is not product-accurate. The real Vidunder Olien label (assets/products-png/skin-oil.png, product-transparent.png) has a straight diagonal white chevron band with a violet accent stripe and fine line hatching. Ours draws a curved arc swoosh, the violet is almost invisible, and the label colour is a pale grey-mint instead of the real deeper teal-sage. This fails the checklist item 'Er Produktfarver korrekte?'. [src/sections/hero/label.js] -> Redraw the band as a straight diagonal chevron, matched to the packshot geometry, with a 2-3px violet stripe on its lower edge and fine parallel hairlines. Sample the label base colour from the hi-res packshot (teal-sage ~#7fa497) and check it under the scene's grade, so the rendered label matches the real carton and bottle.

[src/sections/hero/hero.html .hero-lead] -> Wrap the compound in a no-wrap span, `<span class="hero-nowrap">CBD‑hudpleje</span>` with white-space:nowrap, or use a non-breaking hyphen (U+2011).
