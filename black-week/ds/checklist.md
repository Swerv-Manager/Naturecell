Run this before **any** creative is published.

## Governing principle

> NatureCell®'s visual identity must **always feel calm, exclusive and natural**
> — also when content is AI-generated.

Formats and layouts may vary from post to post; the guide's example is
indicative. But the fundamental principles of **typography, colour use, product
presentation and tone** must always be respected, so the brand appears
consistent across every platform.

## Colour weighting (vægtning af farvebrug)

Pay particular attention to the weighting of the colours:

- The **calm greens and neutral nature tones must dominate** the visual field.
- **Contrast and accent colours are used only discreetly**, to create balance
  and focus.
- The design must always feel **harmonious, airy and premium** — never heavy,
  loud (skrigende) or visually cluttered.

## Logo placement

- Place the logo either **bottom-right**, or **centred top or bottom** —
  depending on format and composition.
- **Prefer the secondary NatureCell® logo** wherever possible, so the visual
  expression stays elegant and discreet without the logo dominating the post.
- A logo is **not required on every creative** if the logo is already clearly
  visible on the products.
- Respect the **safe zone** on all sides.

## Graphic elements

**Bubbles (bobler)** in the brand colours may be used to highlight product
features, use cases, occasions or product names.
- Use them **simply and in a balanced way**, as a *supplement* to the design —
  not as dominating elements.
- Make sure colour, size and placement harmonise with the rest of the layout and
  support NatureCell®'s calm, exclusive, natural expression.
- **Never tilt a bubble that carries a claim or text** — always horizontal.

**Lines (streger)** may be used to pinpoint features on products — they should be
**dotted/dashed** (dottede/prikkede), never solid.

**Icons** may be used where relevant, but **sparingly**.

## Product presentation

When a packshot is centred, centre the **product itself** — the drop shadow is
secondary and must not count as part of the bounding box.
